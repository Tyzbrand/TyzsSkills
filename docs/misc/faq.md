# FAQ

Here you'll find <mark style="color:violet;">**answers**</mark> to the most <mark style="color:violet;">**pertinent questions**</mark>.

<details>

<summary><i class="fa-message-question" style="color:blue;">:message-question:</i>  How can I open the skill menu?</summary>

<i class="fa-message-dots" style="color:blue;">:message-dots:</i> There is a <mark style="color:violet;">**button**</mark> for this when you open the <mark style="color:violet;">**inventory**</mark>! You can also press `I`**.**

</details>

<details>

<summary><i class="fa-message-question" style="color:blue;">:message-question:</i>  How can I earn xp?</summary>

<i class="fa-message-dots" style="color:blue;">:message-dots:</i>  By performing <mark style="color:violet;">**basic actions**</mark>:

* Killing entities
* Breaking blocks
* Eating
* Fishing
* Breeding animals
* Unlocking achievements
* Discovering new biomes and dimensions
* Crafting items
* Sleeping

</details>

<details>

<summary><i class="fa-message-question" style="color:blue;">:message-question:</i>  How can I earn SP ?</summary>

<i class="fa-message-dots" style="color:blue;">:message-dots:</i>  You'll earn SP each time you <mark style="color:violet;">**level up**</mark>. The higher your level, the more you earn! You also earn more <mark style="color:violet;">**every 5 levels**</mark><mark style="color:$primary;">**.**</mark>

</details>

<details>

<summary><i class="fa-message-question" style="color:blue;">:message-question:</i>  Why don't I get all my SP back when I refund my skills?</summary>

<i class="fa-message-dots" style="color:blue;">:message-dots:</i>  For <mark style="color:violet;">**balancing reasons**</mark>, when you refund a skill, you only get back <mark style="color:violet;">**30%**</mark> of the original price (configurable).

</details>

<details>

<summary><i class="fa-message-question" style="color:blue;">:message-question:</i>  There is any incompatibilities?</summary>

<i class="fa-message-dots" style="color:blue;">:message-dots:</i>  There are <mark style="color:violet;">**no known**</mark> mod incompatibilities at this time.

</details>

<details>

<summary><i class="fa-message-question" style="color:blue;">:message-question:</i>  How configurable is the mod?</summary>

<i class="fa-message-dots" style="color:blue;">:message-dots:</i>  The mod is <mark style="color:violet;">**99%**</mark> Data-Driven. It means that you can configure <mark style="color:violet;">**almost everything**</mark> you want! ([more details](../))

</details>

<details>

<summary><i class="fa-message-question" style="color:blue;">:message-question:</i>  Can I create an addon?</summary>

<i class="fa-message-dots" style="color:blue;">:message-dots:</i>  Sure! The mod includes an <mark style="color:violet;">**API**</mark> you can use to build clean, <mark style="color:violet;">**native addons**</mark>. ([more details](../#java-api))

</details>

<details>

<summary><i class="fa-message-question" style="color:blue;">:message-question:</i>  Is the mod compatible with KubeJS?</summary>

<i class="fa-message-dots" style="color:blue;">:message-dots:</i>  <mark style="color:violet;">**Fully**</mark>. It features dedicated <mark style="color:violet;">**events**</mark>, <mark style="color:violet;">**bindings**</mark>, and <mark style="color:violet;">**classes**</mark> for KubeJS integration. ([more details](../#kubejs-integration))

</details>

<details>

<summary><i class="fa-message-question" style="color:blue;">:message-question:</i>  Is the mod translated into multiple languages?</summary>

<i class="fa-message-dots" style="color:blue;">:message-dots:</i>  <mark style="color:violet;">**Yes**</mark>! Tyz's Skills already includes <mark style="color:violet;">**several translations**</mark> (some of which are <mark style="color:violet;">**partial**</mark>):

If you want to <mark style="color:violet;">**contribute**</mark> and translate the mod into your <mark style="color:violet;">**own native language**</mark>, please feel free to join our [crowdin](https://crowdin.com/project/tyzs-skills) project!

</details>

<details>

<summary><i class="fa-message-question" style="color:blue;">:message-question:</i>  What is the future vision for this mod?</summary>

<i class="fa-message-dots" style="color:blue;">:message-dots:</i>  Our <mark style="color:violet;">**goal**</mark> is to keep Tyz's Skills as a perfectly balanced <mark style="color:violet;">**plug-and-play**</mark> experience, while simultaneously turning it into a <mark style="color:violet;">**powerful engine**</mark> where anyone can easily create their own <mark style="color:violet;">**custom skills**</mark>, <mark style="color:violet;">**events**</mark>, and <mark style="color:violet;">**mechanics**</mark>.

</details>
